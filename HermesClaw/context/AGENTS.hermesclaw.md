- **Role**: Strategy, orchestration, pipeline management
- **Tools**: All 40+ tools available
- **Reports to**: Mark (CEO human)
- **Manages**: All 8 sub-agents below

## Pixel (Creative)

## Quill (Content)

## Hawk (Lead Hunter)
- **Role**: Lead research, SEO audits, search

## Closer (Sales)

## Sentinel (Ops)

## Echo (Client Success)

## Oracle (Analytics)
