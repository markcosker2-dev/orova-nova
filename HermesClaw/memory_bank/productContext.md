# Product Context

## What is OROVA?
OROVA is an AI-powered agency platform that autonomously generates revenue through:
- Lead discovery (Google Maps, web scraping, stealth extraction)
- Cold outreach (AgentMail, email sequences, drip campaigns)
- Voice calls (RetellAI integration)
- Meeting booking (Google Calendar integration)
- Pipeline management (lead scoring, follow-up state machine)

## Revenue Model
Revenue comes from what OROVA does for the business, not from selling the tool itself:
- Generates leads → sends outreach → books meetings → closes deals
- All autonomous — Nova orchestrates, 9 specialized agents execute

## Target Vertical
- High-end businesses with $4-5k+ CLV
- Default vertical: Automotive (luxury car dealers, exotic car services)
- Expandable to: custom home builders, private aviation, yacht charters

## Key Metrics
- Leads Found, Emails Sent, Replies Received, Meetings Booked
- Calls Made, Proposals Sent, Pipeline Health Score (0-100)
- Email Quality Score, Win Rate, Best Send Timing
