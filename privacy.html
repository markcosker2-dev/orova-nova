Scope of this notice

This notice covers the currently documented Google-account Drive authorization and its use for Nova backup and recovery, including either registered MikeBot client, hermesclaw-drive-backup or OROVA. It also describes how restored records can enter broader Nova operations. This site offers no public account signup.

The current owner-account permission check lists app-specific Drive file access, not Gmail or Calendar. This is not an assertion that every historical client, token or other integration is inactive. Additional Google-account integrations require a separate permission and data-handling review before use.

What is accessed and why

Google Drive access uses drive.file for application-created or application-accessible files. The tools process file names, identifiers, folder relationships, file size and sharing metadata, and backup contents for creation, listing, authorized download, integrity checking and restoration.

Backup contents can include business contact information, prospect status, conversations, internal activity, consent, suppression and approval records. A partial recovery may omit records; neither this notice nor a successful transfer means it is a complete snapshot.

Where information goes

Google stores the backup files in the owner's Drive. Authorized recovery copies may be stored on the owner's computer and restored into Nova on Render. The owner and explicitly approved operators may inspect records for recovery, operations or security. OAuth credentials belong in access-restricted settings or private local files, never this public website.

The isolated backup transport uploads to Google and downloads for authorized recovery; it does not send backup contents to an LLM. After restoration, however, selected operational information may enter other Nova functions. CRM projection can copy business contact and status fields to owner-controlled Google Sheets using a separate service-account connection.

Nova's conversational and drafting functions can send an operator's submitted text, recent conversation context and selected pipeline summaries to a configured AI provider, and return responses through Telegram. Its code supports Groq, Google's Gemini and OpenRouter or an operator-configured compatible endpoint. Actual providers and settings depend on the deployed configuration. This notice does not certify provider retention settings, a training opt-out or zero third-party processing.

Do not put credentials, private client material or Google-derived personal data into an unreviewed AI route. Before using such data for AI processing, the operator must review the actual provider, account settings, purpose and required consent. This notice alone does not implement a technical filter or authorize a new transfer.

Retention, deletion and revocation

There is no promised fixed deletion deadline. Copies may remain in Drive, local recovery storage and Nova's operating database until removed or affected by configured retention or hosting behavior. Retention differs by software version: legacy backup code uses rolling retention; the prepared replacement disables automatic pruning by default and uses Drive Trash when expressly enabled. Do not assume the prepared version is already deployed.

Revoking MikeBot in your Google Account stops future access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete retained Drive files, local copies, restored records or separately connected services. Contact the owner to identify and remove relevant copies. We may request proportionate identity or account-ownership confirmation before releasing or deleting records. Deletion must also account for suppression and consent records needed to prevent further contact.
