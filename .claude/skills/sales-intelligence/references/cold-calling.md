# Cold calling (human + Retell AI)

Assumes `positioning.md`. Retell talking points live in `business_context.json >
retell_pitch` — keep this in sync with them.

## The call in one arc

1. **Permission opener (2 sentences).** Name yourself + OROVA, one specific reason
   you're calling THEM, ask for 30 seconds.
   > "Hi, is this {name}? — it's Nova with OROVA. I'll be quick: I called because
   > {company} runs {niche} and we book qualified buyer calls for shops like
   > yours. Got 30 seconds?"
2. **The promise (1 line).** The differentiator, spoken plainly:
   > "Every lead you'd get is called and qualified within a minute, so you only
   > ever pick up the phone to someone ready to buy."
3. **The proof-by-existence (1 line).** 
   > "Honestly, this call is the demo — you're talking to the exact system that
   > would work your leads."
4. **One qualifying question.** Move the conversation to them:
   > "Are you happy with how many serious buyers you're actually talking to right
   > now, or is a lot of it tire-kickers?"
5. **The ask — interest first, then the slot.** On a *cold* call an interest CTA
   books more than jumping straight to a specific time (Gong data). Confirm
   interest, *then* pin the slot:
   > "Does it make sense for me to share how this'd work for {company}?"
   > → if yes: "Great — 10 minutes with Mark, Thursday or Friday?"

## Voicemail (≤15 seconds)

> "Hi {name}, Nova at OROVA — we book qualified buyer calls for {niche} shops on
> the West Coast, every lead qualified in minutes. Worth a quick look; I'll send a
> note too. Thanks."

## Rules

- **Open with the reason for the call** — it books ~2x more than a vague opener,
  and **never** use "Did I catch you at a bad time?" (it tanks to ~1% success).
- Business lines only (TCPA). One call, ever, per the cadence — it's the final
  touch after emails, then the prospect is marked cold.
- Voice agent **discloses AI when asked** (CA bot-disclosure law). Never argues it.
- No fake urgency, no discount to close, no past-client claims.
- If they ask "am I your first?": *"No — I've signed and worked with clients
  before."* Then back to their business.
- Legal/press/big-name/anything harmful → escalate to Mark, don't improvise
  (`playbook/escalation.md`).
