# Follow-up sequencing

The cadence is a **hard cap from `outreach-voice.md`** — five touches per prospect,
ever. Never exceed it; never run parallel channels as "multi-channel."

## The sequence

| # | Touch | Timing | Intent | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial email | Day 0 | Hook + value + ask (see `cold-email.md`) | ≤75 words |
| 2 | Follow-up 1 | +2–3 days | Add one *new* angle, not "just bumping" | ≤50 words |
| 3 | Follow-up 2 | +3–4 days | Social-proof-by-existence / a different vertical hook | ≤50 words |
| 4 | Follow-up 3 (break-up) | +4–5 days | "Last note" — and it must be TRUE | ≤40 words |
| 5 | Cold call | after break-up | One call, the arc in `cold-calling.md` | — |
| — | Mark cold | after the call | Never contact again | — |

Each email is a **different day**. Escalation email→call is sequential and spaced
(approved; not bombardment).

## Per-touch craft

- **FU1** — new value, not a nag: *"One more thought, {name} — the reason this fits
  {niche} specifically is speed-to-lead: every inquiry called in minutes. Worth 10?"*
- **FU2** — proof-by-existence: *"{name} — quick reframe: the outreach you're
  reading is the product. If your leads got worked this fast, would that move the
  needle for {company}?"*
- **FU3 / break-up** — clean exit, no guilt-trip, no fake deadline:
  > "{name}, I'll leave it here — last note from me. If qualifying every lead in
  > minutes ever becomes worth a look for {company}, I'm one reply away. — Nova"
  Nothing may send after a break-up. If the system would, that's a bug — flag it.

## Reply handling

Any reply exits the sequence into `objection-handling.md`. A HOT/interested reply
routes to a booking-link reply (approval-gated unless `REPLIES_AUTOPILOT=1`) — see
`business_context.json` and the reply lane. Positive intent → offer two specific
slots, don't ask "when works for you?"
