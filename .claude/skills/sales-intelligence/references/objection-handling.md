# Objection handling

The response library. Each answer: **validate → (optionally) label the emotion →
reframe to the differentiator → secondary ask.** Never argue, never discount,
never claim past clients.

- **Validate first** — "Fair," "Totally understand," "Thanks for flagging that."
  Skipping this triggers hang-ups.
- **Label the emotion** when you can read it — "Sounds like you're slammed right
  now" / "Feels like you've been burned before." Naming it makes the prospect far
  more likely to accept the next ask (Gong cold-call data).
- **Reframe** to the OROVA differentiator (qualified conversations, not leads).
- **Secondary ask, not silence** — a forward question ("When's better?", "Who
  handles this?", "What's the priority right now?") keeps the call alive.

| They say | Respond (reframe in bold) |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive / what's it cost?" | "Fair — it's $4k a month for the ads-and-creative package, $5k if we also qualify every lead for you. **The point isn't cost, it's that you stop paying your team to chase tire-kickers** — you only talk to buyers ready to move. Want the 10-minute version?" |
| "Am I your first client?" | "No — I've signed and worked with clients before. **What matters for you is the system: every lead called and qualified in minutes.** Want to see it on your numbers?" |
| "Send me proof / references." | "Due to our privacy policy we don't share client information — **and that same privacy protects you**: your numbers never get shown to prospects who don't buy. The proof you can see is this outreach — you're already inside the machine that would work your leads." |
| "We already tried an agency / got burned." | "Most agencies hand you a pile of leads to chase — that's the part that burned you. **We do the opposite: every lead is called and qualified before it reaches you.** Different product. Worth 10 minutes to compare?" |
| "Just email me the info." | "Happy to — I'll send a short note. **One line so it's useful: we book you qualified buyer conversations, not raw leads.** If it lands, a 10-minute call beats a PDF. Which inbox?" |
| "I don't have time." | "That's exactly the problem we remove — **your team's time goes only to ready buyers, not tire-kickers.** Ten minutes now saves the hours later. Thursday or Friday?" |
| "How do I know the leads are good?" | "Because **every single one is called and qualified within minutes** — the unqualified ones never reach you. You literally only see buyers ready to continue." |
| "Not interested." | Accept it cleanly. Log it, honor the cadence cap, mark cold. No pushing. "Understood — I won't keep knocking. If it changes, you know where we are." |

## Hard stops (escalate, don't handle)

Legal threats, press, a big-name/high-value opportunity, refund/cancellation
disputes, or anything harmful → hand to Mark (`playbook/escalation.md`). Do not
negotiate price, terms, or promises beyond the ceiling in `positioning.md`.
