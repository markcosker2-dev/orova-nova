# Positioning — what OROVA actually sells

The shared core every other reference builds on. Read this once; the channel docs
assume it.

## The one-sentence frame

> OROVA hands luxury automotive businesses **conversations with buyers who are
> already qualified** — not a list of leads to chase. Every inquiry is called and
> qualified by AI within minutes, so the owner only ever talks to people ready to
> move. And they've already watched it work — that's exactly how OROVA reached them.

That is "premium revenue growth," not "lead generation." The distinction is the
whole brand:

| Say this (revenue growth) | Never say this (commodity agency) |
|---|---|
| "conversations with ready-to-buy owners" | "we generate leads for you" |
| "every lead qualified in minutes" | "we'll grow your business" |
| "you only talk to people ready to move" | "boost your sales / more customers" |
| "the machine that reached you is the product" | "digital marketing services" |
| "built for luxury automotive, not generalist" | "we work with all kinds of businesses" |

## The differentiator (lead with it, always)

1. **The product demos itself** — the prospect was a lead inside OROVA's own AI
   machine *before* they ever paid. The outreach they're reading is the proof.
2. **Speed-to-lead** — every lead called + qualified within minutes, 24/7.
3. **AI-operated + luxury-focused** — not a spray-and-pray generalist shop.

Fully-in-our-control promise to anchor on: *"every lead gets called and qualified
within minutes."* Make reasonable, benchmark-flavored forward promises; never a
guaranteed number on a deadline.

## ICP (who this is for)

Luxury / premium **automotive** businesses on the US West Coast that can afford
$4–5k/mo plus ad spend: exotic & luxury **dealers**, exotic **rentals**, high-end
**detailers / ceramic coating**, **wrap / PPF** shops, **performance / tuning**,
**restoration**. Decision-maker is the owner/GM, reachable on a business line.
(ICP stays mixed per owner 2026-07-13 — automotive is the lead vertical, not the
only one; home builders / luxury RE / high-ticket services still qualify.)

## What we sell (keep pricing exact)

- **Package 1 — $4k/mo:** Meta (FB+IG) lead gen + AI ad creatives. Client handles
  the leads. Ad spend separate (~$2–2.5k/mo).
- **Package 2 — $5k/mo:** P1 + Retell AI qualification + CRM kept current +
  appointment booking. This is where the "you only talk to qualified buyers"
  promise lives. Tiers: 1/3/6-month (see `business_context.json > services`).

For a cold first touch with no case study, **lead with P1**; only surface P2's AI
qualification if they ask "what do you do with the leads." (per `outreach`.)
