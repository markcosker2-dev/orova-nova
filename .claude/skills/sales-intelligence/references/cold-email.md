# Cold email

Assumes `positioning.md`. Machine spec lives in `business_context.json > email_rules`
and `> outreach` — this is the craft.

## Structure: Hook → Value → Ask (≤75 words total)

- **Hook (1 line):** something specific and *true* about THEIR business. Not
  "I came across your site" — name the car on their lot, the service, the city.
- **Value (1 line):** one relevant sentence of the differentiator, in their terms.
  First touch → the SAMPLE, not a package: *"we've already built the AI lead
  qualifier — can I have it call you?"* (ADR-0017). Name no package and no
  price. Which one fits is a diagnosis made later, on the call.
- **Ask (1 line):** low-pressure, time-boxed. "Worth a 15-minute call?" Never
  "hop on a quick call to discuss synergies."

## Subjects (pick, don't stack)

`Quick question about {company}` · `Thought of {company}` · `{company} + OROVA` ·
`Idea for {company}`. Lowercase-ish, no emojis, no "FREE", no clickbait.

## Rules

- Under 75 words. If it's longer, cut — brevity reads as senior.
- Lead with them, not us. The first noun should be their business.
- No spam triggers: free, guaranteed, act now, make money, limited time, $$$.
- Identify OROVA clearly. Signature: `Nova @ OROVA`.
- One ask, one link at most. No attachments.
- Nova doesn't volunteer being AI; never denies it if asked.

## Templates (luxury automotive)

**Exotic / luxury dealer**
> Subject: Thought of {company}
> Hi {name} — saw the {specific_model} you've got listed at {company}. We run
> Meta ads for West-Coast luxury dealers, and every lead gets called and
> qualified within minutes, so you only talk to buyers actually ready to move.
> Worth 15 minutes to see if it fits {company}?
> Nova @ OROVA

**Detailer / ceramic / PPF**
> Subject: {company} + OROVA
> Hi {name} — {company}'s {specific_service} work looks sharp. We fill high-end
> detail/PPF calendars with owners who already want the premium package, and our
> AI qualifies every inquiry in minutes so you're not chasing tire-kickers.
> Open to a quick look?
> Nova @ OROVA

**Exotic rental**
> Subject: Quick question about {company}
> Hi {name} — is {company} booked out on weekends or still filling dates? We put
> luxury rentals in front of the right renters and qualify every inquiry fast, so
> your team only handles serious bookings. Worth a 15-min call?
> Nova @ OROVA

Swap `{specific_*}` for something you actually verified (enrichment gives you the
site/inventory). A wrong specific is worse than none — if you can't verify, use a
category hook ("your PPF work"), never invent a detail.
