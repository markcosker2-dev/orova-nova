# Luxury automotive — per-vertical hooks

Vertical-specific language so outreach sounds like it's from someone who knows the
business. Pair each hook with a *verified* specific from enrichment. Keep the
luxury register — these owners are allergic to discount-agency tone.

## Exotic / luxury dealers
- **Pain:** floor traffic is unqualified; sales team burns hours on tire-kickers
  and lowball "just looking" inquiries on six-figure cars.
- **Hook language:** inventory-specific ("the {model} you've got listed"), buyer
  intent, allocation/consignment, out-of-state buyer logistics.
- **Promise fit:** every inquiry qualified in minutes → the closer only speaks to
  funded, ready buyers.

## Exotic / luxury rentals
- **Pain:** weekend/date gaps, no-shows, unqualified renters, insurance-risk
  bookings.
- **Hook:** "booked out on weekends or still filling dates?", event/wedding/photo
  shoot demand, fleet utilization.
- **Promise fit:** qualified booking inquiries only; fewer flaky reservations.

## Detailing / ceramic coating
- **Pain:** price-shoppers wanting a $50 wash; calendar full of low-ticket work.
- **Hook:** premium package (multi-stage paint correction, ceramic, PPF add-on),
  the specific finish work on their site/IG.
- **Promise fit:** fill the calendar with owners who already want the premium tier.

## Wrap / PPF
- **Pain:** quote-and-ghost, comparison shoppers, install-slot gaps.
- **Hook:** color-change wraps, full-front / track-pack PPF, the marque they wrap.
- **Promise fit:** qualified install bookings, deposit-ready.

## Performance / tuning
- **Pain:** enthusiasts who ask endless questions and never book dyno time.
- **Hook:** platform-specific (Porsche/BMW/Corvette builds), stage kits, dyno.
- **Promise fit:** talk only to owners ready to book a build, not forum browsers.

## Restoration
- **Pain:** long sales cycles, budget-mismatch inquiries, high-value trust barrier.
- **Hook:** marque/era specialty, concours-quality, the current build on their feed.
- **Promise fit:** qualified, budget-aware project inquiries.

## Register rules
- Elevated, specific, peer-to-peer — like a knowledgeable enthusiast, not a rep.
- Reference craft and marques, not "your business needs more customers."
- Never cheapen with "deals", "cheap", "boost", "explode your sales".
