## 1. Project overview & goals

**OROVA** is a marketing agency that runs **Meta ads for luxury/premium US
West-Coast SMBs**. **"Nova"** is its autonomous lead-gen agent. **The single goal
right now: land the FIRST paying client.** Everything serves that.

- **Packages:** P1 **$4,000/mo** (Meta lead-gen + AI creatives); P2 **$5,000/mo**
  (P1 + Retell AI cold-call lead qualification + booking). 1-month trial, then
  1/3/6-month terms. Ad spend is **client-paid** (direct to Meta).
- **ICP (luxury, West Coast CA/OR/WA/NV/AZ):** exotic/luxury auto (dealers,
  rentals, restoration), custom home builders / high-end remodeling, luxury real
  estate, high-ticket services. (The plan recommends *dropping* private aviation /
  yacht charter for a solo cold-outreach motion — decision-makers there are family
  offices, not reachable by cold email.)
- **Funnel:** hunt → cold email → (no reply) Retell cold call → book with Mark →
  Google Meet → agreement + invoice (Wise/ACH) → onboarding.
- **Margins:** realistically **~97%** at 1/3/10 clients (per-client cost $35–150/mo).

## 2. Architecture & tech stack

- **`app/` — Nova**: Python 3.11 / FastAPI, deployed on **Render free tier**
  (`orova-nova.onrender.com`). SQLite state, backed up to Google Drive. **9
  scheduled worker lanes** (`app/worker.py`) via the `schedule` lib in a daemon
  thread started from the FastAPI lifespan:
  1. Fast lane (2 min) — approvals + execute approved calls
  2. Slow lane (60 min) — **lead hunt** + cold email + drip enroll
  3. Reply+drip (5 min) — reply monitor → **booking funnel** + drip follow-ups
  4. Cold-lead escalation (30 min) — Retell cold call (DNC-gated)
  5. Cloud backup (3 h) — SQLite → Google Drive
  6. CEO brief (daily 17:00 UTC)
  7. Health check (2 h)
  8. Self-improvement (6 h) — champion/challenger + 2 learning loops
  9. Drip sender (1 h)
- **`electron/` + `src/` — HermesClaw**: desktop cockpit (Electron/TS, built on
  **OpenClaw**). `HermesClaw/` = reference mirrors only. **Not the revenue path.**
- **`mission-control/` — dashboard** served by the FastAPI app (static HTML/JS).
- **`vault/` — Obsidian knowledge base** = the agents' shared brain. Synced from
  production by `scripts/vault_pull.py`.
- **LLM routing** (`app/core/ai_client.py`): Groq `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` →
  native Gemini 2.5-flash → OpenRouter free models. Retell voice = gpt-4.1-mini.

**Constraints (hard):** Render free tier = **no headless browser**, **SMTP ports
blocked** (email verify = MX only), **512 MB RAM**, **25s enrichment ceiling**
(below Render's 30s kill). **`httpx` pinned at 0.27.2** (starlette 0.27 TestClient
vs mcp/ollama — see requirements.txt). Everything must be **$0 / free-tier**.
