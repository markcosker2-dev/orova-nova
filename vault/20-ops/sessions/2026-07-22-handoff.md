## 4. Hard constraints / gotchas
- **512MB free tier is the binding limit.** Batch reenrich (waterfall page
  crawls) OOM-restarts the instance. **Run `POST /api/actions/reenrich-leads`
  at `limit 1`** (safe, verified). Batch throughput needs the paid tier.
  Drive now restores after an OOM, so it's no longer catastrophic.
- **Free-tier AI quota:** Groq + Gemini free tiers 429 together under load;
  OpenRouter (free models, now fixed) is the 3rd tier. Chat can still hit a
  moment where all three are exhausted → Nova returns a graceful
  "rate-limited, try again" (not a crash).
- **Deploys wipe SQLite;** Drive restore + Sheets fallback repopulate.
- **Never boot the app locally with prod `.env`** — startup registers the
  production Telegram webhook and hijacks it. Use a static stub for UI.
