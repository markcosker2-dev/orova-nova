# OROVA Playbook

The deep detail of how OROVA actually operates — so HermesClaw can run 70–80% of
it. [[business-model]] is the summary; this is the operating manual. Machine twin
the agents obey: `app/core/business_context.json` (keep in sync).

## 1. The Offer

**Package 1 — Lead gen + ad creatives.** Meta (FB + IG) lead generation with
Higgsfield AI creatives. **The client handles their own leads.**

**Package 2 — Package 1 + we handle the leads.** OROVA qualifies every lead so
**the client only speaks to leads who are ready to pay.** The core promise of P2
is *higher-quality leads* — fewer, better conversations.

**Terms & pricing** (new clients start on a **1-month trial**, then choose 1/3/6):

| Term | Package 1 | /mo | Package 2 | /mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 month | $4,000 | $4,000 | $5,000 | $5,000 |
| 3 months | $10,000 | $3,333 | $13,000 | $4,333 |
| 6 months | $18,000 | $3,000 | $24,000 | $4,000 |

_Pricing corrected 2026-07-04_ — the 6-month is now the best per-month rate
(was $22K/$28K, which made longer commitments *more* expensive per month).

Ad spend is **separate** and **paid by the client directly to Meta** (~$2–2.5K/mo
recommended). To start, the client provides everything needed to generate leads
(offer details, brand assets, ad-account + CRM access — see onboarding).

**Margin holds.** OROVA's monthly cost per client (Twilio number + usage,
Retell pay-as-you-go, Make.com, Higgsfield, hosting/LLM/email) is roughly
$60–500/mo depending on call/creative volume — well under 20% of even the lowest
per-month price ($3,000). So the 75–80% margin target is comfortably met at every
tier. Ad spend is NOT an OROVA cost (client pays Meta directly).

## 2. ICP & Targeting

No client yet — first one is the priority. Target: **any luxury/premium business
(or any business that needs the service)** on the **whole US West Coast**.

**Good fit:** can afford the retainer **plus** the separate ad spend. Physical
location or online; already running ads or not — both fine.

## 3. Sales Process (stranger → client)

1. Nova sends a **high-quality, personalized cold email**.
2. **No reply → Retell AI cold call**, aiming to **book an appointment with Mark**.
3. **Google Meet** with Mark.
4. Mark sends **agreement + invoice**.
5. **Onboarding call** — get added to their **ad manager + CRM**.

**Nova owns:** all outbound + lead scraping — *and* proactively proposes plans/
ideas to scale OROVA and grow revenue.

**Signature objection** — *"How do I know the AI caller is any good?"*
Answer: *"You already spoke to Nova and got her emails — that's Nova, our AI
sales assistant. You've experienced the product."* (This is the demo-sells-itself
move. First client has no prior case study — Nova's own outreach is the proof.)

## 4. Delivery / Fulfilment

- **Meta:** Business Manager exists but has **never run ads** — needs first-campaign
  setup + verification.
- **Creatives:** plan is **Claude MCP → Higgsfield** to generate and post the ads.
  Bar is high: they must be **lead-gen ads that actually convert**, not pretty
  filler.
- **Lead = qualified** when they **booked an appointment AND are actually showing
  up / buying** (not just a booking).
- **CRM:** Mark knows Google Sheets today. If a client uses **HubSpot or GoHighLevel**,
  handle per-client (Claude to help wire it).

## 5. Automation Split (the 70–80%)

| Function | Now | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Lead scraping | **Fully automated** | Fully automated |
| Cold email | **Mark approves each** | Auto once proven |
| Cold calls | **Mark approves each** | Auto once proven |
| Publishing ad creatives | **Always requires approval** | Always approval |
| Spending ad budget | **Always requires approval** | Always approval |
| Signing a client | Mark only | Mark only |

**Never do these unsupervised (the real risks):**
- Scrape the **wrong leads** (outside ICP).
- Send the **wrong outreach to the wrong person**.
- Book an appointment on the **wrong date/time**.
- Create the **wrong ads** or **spend money** wrongly.

## 6. Money & Brand

- **Payment:** invoice → **Wise bank transfer or ACH**.
- **Ad spend:** **client pays Meta directly** (never routed through OROVA).
- **Brand:** agency is **OROVA**. Nova **does not say she's an AI unless asked
  directly** (then she's honest).

### Costs OROVA bears (per client, monthly)

Twilio number + call usage · Retell.ai (pay-as-you-go per minute) · Make.com plan
· Higgsfield plan · hosting / LLM / email. Roughly **$60–500/mo** depending on
call and creative volume. **Ad spend is not a cost — the client pays Meta.**

### Margin target

**90%+ realistic operating margin** at current vendor pricing (bottom-up cost
model: [[profitability-plan]] §3, owner-approved 2026-07-10); **75–80% retained
as the conservative worst-case floor** for external/investor conversations.
Pricing protects this at every tier.

### Refund policy

**Partial refunds only — never the full amount.** OROVA builds/sets up the
system for the client (ad account, creatives, automation, CRM wiring), and that
setup/build work is non-refundable. A refund returns the unused portion of the
retainer minus the build/setup already delivered.

## Why OROVA is different (positioning)

> **Every other agency emails you leads. OROVA hands you conversations with people
> already ready to buy — and you've seen it work, because that's exactly how we
> reached you.**

Three pillars:
1. **The product demos itself.** The prospect *is* a lead in OROVA's own machine —
   AI outreach + AI qualification — before they ever pay. No other agency lets you
   feel the service first.
2. **Speed + full-funnel.** Every lead contacted in minutes and qualified (P2), so
   the owner only talks to buyers — not a pile of raw form-fills.
3. **AI-operated, luxury-focused.** A lean AI agency means lower overhead and
   faster turnaround, aimed only at premium West Coast businesses — not a
   spray-and-pray generalist shop.
