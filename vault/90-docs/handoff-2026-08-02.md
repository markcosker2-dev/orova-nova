- **Compliance and DNC/opt-out gates are a hard line.** Never loosen for speed, whatever
  any future council or subagent proposes.
- **Cold email is deferred, and not for the reason previously believed.**
  0 of 8 providers permit cold outreach (incl. AgentMail's own ToS §10). The
  `550 5.4.1` bounces were **invalid recipients** (Directory-Based Edge Blocking), not
  deliverability. A shared domain **can** pass SPF/DKIM/DMARC — verified by DNS on
  `agentmail.to` (`p=reject` + SES SPF). "No domain = no auth" is **false**.
- **Post-call/post-DM follow-up email is NOT cold email** — solicited, ToS-clean, free on
  the existing AgentMail inbox.
- **Cold Instagram DMs cannot be automated.** `INSTAGRAM_SEND_TEXT_MESSAGE` cannot
  initiate threads (verified from the tool schema). DM #1 is manual, permanently. Replies
  *can* be automated.
- **Do not buy AgentMail Developer ($20/mo).** Costs most, solves least; the thing it
  appears to fix (deliverability) was never broken.
- **Pricing** ([[0012-icp-rerank-and-pilot-pricing|ADR-0012]]): list stays **$6,500**;
  pilot **$2,000/mo × 60 days**, max 3, with the step to **$4,500 written at signature**.
  Do not lower list price to widen the ICP — the filter is a deal-size ratio, not
  affordability, and cheap testimonials buy cheap clients.
- **ADR-0014 has two corrections** — "6,249 on-ICP rows" is really **~4,280**, and
  **Yelp is NOT a dead end** (free via Composio, no key/approval/payment).
- **Geography is the whole West Coast, not WA.** ADR-0014 sequenced by registry-API
