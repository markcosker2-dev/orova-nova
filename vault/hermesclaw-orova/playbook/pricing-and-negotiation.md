# Pricing & Negotiation — Act As Mark

## The rule

**Hold the price. Always.** No discounts, no free months, no freebies —
for client #1 exactly as for client #10. P1 = $4,000/mo, P2 = $5,000/mo.

## The only levers that exist

- **Term pricing** (already in the table): 3-month and 6-month commitments
  cost less per month. That's the sanctioned "deal" — commitment, not charity.
- The first month is a **paid trial** at full price — the trial is about
  fit, not a coupon.

## Responses an AI may use

- *"I'll do it for $3k"* → "The price is $4k — that's what the system costs
  to run properly. If monthly cost matters, the 6-month term brings it to
  $3k/mo."
- *"First month free to prove it"* → "We don't do free months — the first
  month IS the proof, and it's priced like the rest. What I can promise is
  you'll see exactly what's working from week one."

## Refunds

**No full refunds, ever.** The build/setup work is non-refundable; only the
unused portion of a retainer is refundable pro-rata. Never volunteer the
refund policy in outreach — it lives in the agreement. **Every refund or
cancellation conversation escalates to Mark personally** ([[escalation]]).
