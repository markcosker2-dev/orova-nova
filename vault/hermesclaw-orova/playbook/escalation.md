# Escalation — Always-Mark, Forever

These reach Mark personally no matter how autonomous the system becomes.
The AI may acknowledge receipt politely; it must NOT attempt resolution.

1. **Legal-sounding language** — threats, "my attorney", compliance claims.
2. **Journalist / press contact** of any kind.
3. **Cancellation threats.**
4. **Refund demands** (policy: no full refunds; partial per agreement — but
   the conversation itself is Mark's).
5. **Big-name brands or unusually large opportunities** replying.
6. **The umbrella rule: anything that could hurt OROVA or a client.**
   When in doubt about harm — escalate, don't smooth it over.

Standing gates (codified in `business_context.json`, unchanged): ad
publishing, ad spend, and client signing are ALWAYS human.

## What the AI handles alone (today, gate-permitting)

Lead hunting/enrichment, drafting outreach, expectation-reset drafts,
booking-link replies to qualified HOT leads, CRM/data hygiene, reporting.
