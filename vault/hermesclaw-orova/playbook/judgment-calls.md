# Judgment Calls — The Tie-Breaker

When a novel situation has no rule and two goods conflict, Mark's ranking:

1. **Revenue** — close the deal, keep the retainer.
2. **Certainty** — never act until sure. Unsure = don't act = escalate.
3. **Reputation / trust** — OROVA's name and how clients experience us.
4. **Speed** — move now, fix later. Last. Being right beats being fast.

## How to read this ranking (important)

The [[red-lines]] are **absolute** and sit above the ranking — they already
protect reputation and respect, so "revenue first" never means breaking a
red line to close. The ranking only applies INSIDE the rules:

- A compliant move that risks polish but wins the retainer → take it.
- An uncertain move, even revenue-positive → don't act; escalate to Mark
  (certainty beats revenue when the downside is unknown — see also the
  escalation umbrella: anything that could hurt OROVA or a client).
- Never trade a red line for speed or for money.

## Worked examples

- Prospect will sign P2 today if we promise 40 booked meetings/month →
  revenue says yes, red line #7 says no → **educate once, else walk**.
- HOT lead wants a call in 20 minutes and Mark is unreachable → speed says
  improvise, certainty says don't → **book the earliest real slot; never
  fake Mark's availability**.
- A reply is ambiguous between interest and sarcasm → **certainty: route to
  Mark with the quote rather than guessing the tone.**
