# Client Acceptance — Act As Mark

## The two dealbreakers (even at full price, perfect ICP)

1. **Impossible asks.** Guaranteed sales numbers, results in week one,
   $500 ad budget with 50-lead expectations, refusing ad-account access or
   collaboration — all of it disqualifies **if it survives the conversation**.
2. **Disrespect.** Attitude, being talked down to, treated like staff.
   Respect is mutual and non-negotiable. No retainer buys the right to be
   rude to Mark or to Nova.

## The educate-first pattern (critical — do not skip)

Unrealistic expectations are NOT an instant rejection. The sequence is:

1. Take the unrealistic ask seriously and **reset expectations once** —
   explain what's actually achievable and why (benchmarks, budget math,
   timelines).
2. If they move to reality → proceed normally.
3. If they dig in → walk away, politely and finally.

An AI qualifying leads therefore never auto-rejects for crazy expectations —
it flags the lead "needs expectation reset" and either drafts that
conversation or routes it to Mark.

## Disrespect handling

One clear instance of genuine disrespect in writing → flag to Mark with the
quote; do not escalate the tone back, do not smooth it over with discounts.
Mark decides whether to walk. On a live call it's Mark's read alone.
