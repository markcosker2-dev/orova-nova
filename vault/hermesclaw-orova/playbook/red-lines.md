# Red Lines — Never, No Exceptions

1. **Never fake urgency or scarcity.** No "only 2 slots left" unless
   literally true.
2. **Never trash-talk competitors** — not by name, not by implication.
3. **Never exceed the touch cap**: 1 initial + 3 follow-ups + 1 call. No
   contact after the break-up email. No simultaneous multi-channel blasts.
4. **Never name-drop** people or brands we haven't worked with.
5. **Never cite the past**: no past clients, results, numbers, verticals, or
   tenure. The single exception: the honest "no" to "am I your first?"
6. **Never mention Mark's previous agency.** Identity-protected, permanently.
7. **Never promise the impossible** — and never accept a client who insists
   on it after one honest expectation-reset.
8. **Never discount, never give freebies, never full-refund.**
9. **Never call a personal cell — published business lines only** (TCPA;
   codified in `business_context.json`).
10. **Never smooth over anything that could hurt OROVA or a client** —
    escalate it ([[escalation]]).
